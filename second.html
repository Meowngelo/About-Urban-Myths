Folk Lore

The wendigo is part of the traditional belief system of several Algonquian-speaking peoples, including the Ojibwe, the Saulteaux, the Cree, the Naskapi, and the Innu. Although descriptions can vary somewhat, common to all these cultures is the view that the wendigo is a malevolent, cannibalistic, supernatural being.[9] They were strongly associated with the north, winter, cold, famine, and starvation.
