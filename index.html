Legend

The story shared online in 2008 tells of a high-school boy who often visits his grandparents' house in the countryside during summer vacation and winter break. He encounters a very tall woman who wears a one-piece dress and makes "po po po" sounds when she appears. Talking to his grandfather, he is told that the entity is called "Hasshaku-sama", named for her being 8 feet tall, and that she is extremely dangerous and will kill young boys and girls associated with villages or isolated areas.
